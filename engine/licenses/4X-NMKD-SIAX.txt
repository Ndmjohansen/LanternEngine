4x-NMKD-Siax (CX, 200k) by NMKD
https://openmodeldb.info/models/4x-NMKD-Siax-CX
ncnn conversion: https://github.com/upscayl/custom-models (models/4x_NMKD-Siax_200k.param, .bin)

Not shipped with Lantern Engine. The launcher downloads the ncnn conversion (checked by SHA-256)
when a player builds the HD menus, and runs it through realesrgan-ncnn-vulkan
(licenses/REAL-ESRGAN.txt) on that player's own game files.
